Plant MAE-prompt: Lesion-aware Self-supervised Pretraining with Hierarchical
   Prompt Tuning for Cross-crop Overlapped Disease Detection in Corn and
                              Tomato Leaves

                                            Amandeep Kaur1*                    Archana Sandhu1

                 1
                  Maharishi Markandeshwar Institute of Computer Technology & Business Management,
                                     Mullana-Ambala, Haryana, 133207, India
                             * Corresponding author’s Email: kangaman269@gmail.com


  Abstract: Inter-class visual overlap is one of the critical factors inhibiting automated detection of foliar diseases in
  corn (Zea mays) and tomato (Solanum lycopersicum), where morphologically similar symptoms across crop species,
  including Cercospora Gray Leaf Spot in corn and Septoria Leaf Spot in tomato, are misclassified by conventional deep
  learning classifiers. Current methods also have the disadvantage of significantly reduced performance under field
  conditions (not in the laboratory). To address these problems, this paper presents PlantMAE-Prompt, a new three-
  phase deep neural network evaluated on PlantVillage and PlantDoc. Phase 1 introduces a Lesion-Aware Masking
  (LAM) strategy, biasing Masked Autoencoder (MAE) pretraining toward pathologically informative regions using
  CIELAB color-space saliency priors, compelling the Vision Transformer (ViT) encoder to acquire disease-
  discriminative features. Phase 2 proposes Hierarchical Prompt Tuning (HPT) which decomposes learnable prompt
  tokens into shared, crop-specific, and disease-specific levels with orthogonality penalty, training only 2.1% (~1.8M)
  of the total parameters of the frozen encoder. Phase 3 uses Overlap-Aware Contrastive Fine-Tuning (OACFT) that
  detects confusable disease pairs from the evolving confusion matrix and resolves them via pair-specific prompts and
  adaptive margin-based contrastive optimization. To eliminate train–test mismatch, the same top-K prompt evaluation
  rule used at inference is also applied during training, ensuring that no privileged label information is exploited. It has
  been experimentally demonstrated that, compared to state-of-the-art methods, PlantMAE-Prompt has an accuracy of
  98.73 on PlantVillage and 78.64 on the cross-domain PlantVillage→PlantDoc setting, +1.11% and +9.22 higher,
  respectively, and is about 52-fold more parameter-efficient (1.8M vs Computational cost is also audited with training
  FLOPs, peak GPU memory and inference latency at equal 224x224 input and equal hardware. The complementary
  nature of each suggested element is confirmed by ablation experiments, statistical significance (p < 0.001)
  measurement and t-SNE plots.
  Keywords: Plant disease detection, Masked autoencoder, Vision transformer, Prompt tuning, Contrastive learning,
  Overlapped disease classification, Cross-domain generalization, Self-supervised learning.
