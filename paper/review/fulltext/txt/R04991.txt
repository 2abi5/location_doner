exemplars: a SOTA VLM generates structured reasoning chains, which are subsequently filtered by an LLM-based judge to
ensure quality. Stage 2 performs GRPO-based policy learning, incorporating domain vocabulary construction with proposed
five-tier fuzzy matching to handle linguistic variability, along with a three-component reward function (Format, Answer,
Reasoning) to guide optimization. Group-relative advantage normalization is further applied to enable stable policy updates.
