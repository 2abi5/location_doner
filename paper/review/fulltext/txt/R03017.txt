                                               change policies benefit from high quality spatial and temporal agricultural land cover data
                                               (Karthikeyan et al., 2020). Monitoring of policy impacts is crucial to avoid long-term negative
                                               effects (e.g., unsustainable practices) and, if necessary, to take appropriate corrective actions.
