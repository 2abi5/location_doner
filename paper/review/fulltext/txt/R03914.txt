                                           Accurate daily predictions of cold hardiness in woody plants
                                           are critical in regions where freezing temperatures can damage
                                           dormant buds and reduce seasonal yield. Existing biophysical,
                                           hybrid, and deep learning models have shown high predic-
                                           tive accuracy when trained on local data but remain largely
                                           site-specific. The limited availability of cold hardiness data,
                                           coupled with the lack of principled methods for transferring
                                           cold hardiness predictions to new regions and cultivars, has
                                           limited the broader adoption and practical utility of these ap-
                                           proaches, particularly in data-scarce regions. To address these
                                           limitations, we propose a cold hardiness prediction frame-
                                           work that learns a transferable latent representation by captur-
                                           ing region-specific variation through learned embeddings. To
                                           enable prediction in previously unseen regions, we infer em-
