   Benchmarking and evaluation of foundation models. A sep-
arate line of work evaluates how well geospatial foundation models
generalize. GEO-Bench [12] assembles many tasks but randomly
subsamples image chips, removing the spatial structure needed to
test transfer to new regions. PANGAEA [14] reports that supervised

transfer test, but on a single urban land cover dataset rather than
crop mapping. PhilEO-Bench [4] evaluates only on Sentinel-2 data          Figure 2: USDA Cropland Data Layer ground truth, with the
